# Code of Conduct

K9HOPE adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep discussion focused on making canine blood donation safer and easier. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not acceptable.

This project touches veterinary care. Please do not post real patient, donor or owner details in issues, pull requests or discussions. Use fictional data.
